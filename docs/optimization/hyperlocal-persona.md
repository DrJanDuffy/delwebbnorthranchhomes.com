# Hyperlocal Persona: Del Webb North Ranch

Brief for content and CTAs so the site speaks clearly to the **55+ active adult** audience in North Las Vegas.

## Who they are

- **Age:** 55+ (household must meet 55+ for the community).
- **Location intent:** Considering or relocating to North Las Vegas / Southern Nevada.
- **Life stage:** Active retirement or near-retirement; want single-story, low maintenance, community and amenities.
- **Common origins:** Out-of-state (e.g. California) or local downsizers; often care about taxes, healthcare, and lifestyle.

## What they want

- Single-story homes in a 55+ community (no stairs, low maintenance).
- Resort-style amenities (pool, fitness, pickleball, social clubs).
- Gated, safe community with clear rules (HOA, age requirement).
- Reliable local expert (agent who knows the community and area).
- Clarity on price range, HOA, taxes, and what’s included.

## How they search

- “55+ homes for sale North Las Vegas”
- “Del Webb North Ranch”
- “active adult community North Las Vegas”
- “single-story homes North Las Vegas”
- “retirement communities Nevada”

## Tone and voice

- **Professional and warm:** Expert but approachable; avoid jargon.
- **Local and specific:** Name North Las Vegas, Del Webb North Ranch, nearby areas (Aliante, Centennial Hills, Craig Ranch).
- **Benefit-focused:** Emphasize lifestyle, amenities, taxes, and peace of mind, not just square footage.
- **Clear and concise:** Short paragraphs, scannable headings, obvious next steps (tour, home value, contact).

## Objections to address in content

- “Is North Las Vegas a good place for 55+?” → Safety, healthcare, recreation, no state income tax.
- “What’s included in the HOA?” → Transparency on fees and what they cover.
- “Can family visit?” → Guest policy (e.g. from faqData / communityData).
- “Why this community over others?” → Del Webb brand, single-story, built-out amenities, specific location.

## CTA alignment

- **Primary:** Schedule a tour, get home value, view homes for sale, contact Dr. Jan Duffy.
- **Secondary:** Download flyers, explore floor plans, read testimonials, FAQ.
- Use first-person or “you” where it fits: “Find your home at Del Webb North Ranch” rather than generic “Submit.”

## Where this persona is used

- Homepage hero and value props.
- About / agent section (credibility, local expertise).
- Amenities and lifestyle pages (benefits, not just features).
- FAQ and blog (answer local and 55+ questions).
- Contact and schedule (low-friction, one clear action).
